Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026. Underlens does not collect, transmit or share any data. Everything it handles stays on your Mac.

What Underlens does not do

  • It has no network access. The app cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.
  • The developer never receives your scripts, your voice, your video or any information about how you use the app.

Microphone and speech

Underlens uses the microphone to follow your place in the script. Your speech is recognised on your Mac using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Voice following does not save audio or transcripts: the words it hears are held in memory only long enough to move the script.

The first time you use a language, macOS itself may download a speech model from Apple. That download is made by macOS, not by Underlens, and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Camera and recordings

The camera is used only while the recording window is open. When you press record, Underlens saves a video with sound to the Underlens folder in Movies, or to a folder you choose. Recordings stay on your Mac until you move or delete them.

Scripts and history

Your scripts, settings and take history (duration, pace and review marks, never recorded speech) are stored in the app's private folder on your Mac. Deleting a script in the app deletes its history. Removing the app's data removes all of it.

Files you choose

Underlens reads a file only when you import it, and writes to a folder only when you pick it as the place to save recordings.

Children

Underlens collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include private information.
